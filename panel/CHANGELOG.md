# Changelog

## 0.7.0

- Any language you learn: labels, hints and the word card name the language in your settings instead of always saying English.
- Word dictionaries for 18 more languages (English → Spanish, German, French, Polish, Japanese…), downloaded once on the first word you look up. New setting `languageTutor.downloadDictionaries`.
- Better ranking of word meanings (German "go" → gehen, Russian "die" → умереть).
- When the language you learn has no voice, the panel shows the steps to add one for Windows or macOS.
- On first start your language is taken from VS Code's display language (or the system's).

## 0.6.1

- First public build: side panel, history, plan and selection tabs, read-aloud with word highlighting, English → Russian word meanings.
